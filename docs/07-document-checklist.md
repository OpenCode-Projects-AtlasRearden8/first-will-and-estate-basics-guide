next month, Phase 3 sometime.** Nothing here is urgent unless life makes it urgent

## The supporting-cast documents (gather these, eventually)

These already exist in your life — the checklist is about knowing *where*:

- [ ] Birth certificate(s), marriage/divorce decrees, citizenship/papers as applicable
- [ ] Deed or lease, mortgage statement
- [ ] Vehicle titles
- [ ] Insurance policies (life, health, home, auto) — policy numbers
- [ ] Retirement/financial statements — most recent of each
- [ ] Social Security card(s); Medicare/Medicaid cards if applicable
- [ ] Recent tax return location
- [ ] Password manager master-access plan (who, how) — see digital-legacy-handbook
- [ ] Safe-deposit box: bank, box number, signer list, **key location**
- [ ] Pet records (vaccination history, vet contact)

> Privacy note: this project's repo contains **none** of these, and yours
> shouldn't sit in any version-controlled or broadly synced location. Chapter 8
> covers the right homes for all of it.

## Households with extra moving parts

Some flags that say "Phase 3 = definitely an attorney, not a template":

- Prior marriages, children from other relationships, blended-family dynamics
- Special-needs family members (special-needs trusts are particular)
- You own a business or part of one, or rental property
- Significant assets, multiple states, or property outside the country
- A family member with creditor trouble (inheritance can be exposed)
- You want disinheritance or unequal splits — valid, but needs drafting care
- Immigration status or cross-border family situations

None of these are "you can't plan." They're all "a professional designs this well."
